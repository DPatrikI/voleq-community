# Code of Conduct

VolEq contributors are expected to make participation respectful, safe, and productive.

- Discuss ideas and code without personal attacks.
- Welcome questions and different experience levels.
- Give actionable, evidence-based feedback.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate against, or expose private information about others.
- Respect project scope, licensing, and the consent of people whose audio could be involved in testing.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when behavior undermines these expectations. Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the contact method listed on their GitHub profile. Reports will be handled as confidentially as reasonably possible.
